Three real severities, not a guessed three-way split: **ON_TIME** (live data within a 3-minute tolerance of the learned baseline, or no baseline yet — degrades to ON_TIME rather than fabricating a verdict with nothing to compare against), **DELAYED** (a real minutes-late number beyond tolerance, phrased with that exact number), **NO_LIVE_DATA** (the user's usual candidate has no live arrivals at all — delegates to `rank_routes` across every home/office candidate for a real substitute).
